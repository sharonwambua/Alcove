Alcove is an online portal that connects property managers and tenants under one digital roof. Think of it as a virtual property office that handles the day-to-day communication, money tracking, and maintenance problems that come with renting or running real estate.

Instead of text messages, paper receipts, and logbooks, everything is organized in one clean dashboard.

# What it Does for Landlords 
The landlord side gives owners a complete view of how their buildings are doing without overwhelming them.

Money Tracking (in Kenyan Shillings): It clearly displays rent collected, rent that tenants claim to have paid but needs to be checked against a bank account (Payments to Confirm), and rent that is late (Unpaid Rent Owed).

Simple Billing: Landlords can create a single monthly bill that combines house rent with utility fees (like a water bill) and send it directly to the tenant's app.

Maintenance Work Orders: When something breaks, landlords can view the problem details side-by-side on their screen, mark how urgent it is, and update the status from Looking for a Contractor to In Progress or Fixed.

Targeted Announcements: Landlords can broadcast news to everyone at once, or send a specific message to just one building (for example, alerting only one complex about a water outage).

Visual Reports: It automatically builds visual graphs tracking monthly earnings and building occupancy without needing extra software plug-ins.

# What it Does for Tenants 
The tenant side is built to be clean, calm, and incredibly easy to use so residents can get things done in seconds.

Quick Shortcuts: The main page has big, interactive buttons that immediately route tenants to where they can pay rent, report a repair, or read updates.

Notice Board: Tenants have a dedicated page to read building updates. Critical updates (like an electrical power outage) are highlighted in red so they instantly catch the eye.

Balance Checker: Tenants can instantly see if they owe any money or if their account is fully cleared, keeping things transparent.
